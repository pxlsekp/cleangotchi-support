CleanGotchi Privacy Policy

Updated: 20 November 2026

1. How the app processes information

CleanGotchi processes cleanup and pet records on your Mac. The current app has no developer server receiving your files, pet records, memory information or usage analytics. It has no advertising trackers, cloud sync or app-specific account registration. AI pet conversations also run on supported Macs using a model included in the app.

If you sign up for a feedback email after purchase, Google processes the information listed in section 8 on behalf of Jay, the developer. You can skip the invitation and use all your purchased features. External privacy and support pages and Apple purchases are also subject to the policies of their respective services.

3. Information stored on your Mac

  • Pet species, nickname, growth, birth/graduation dates and current selection.
  • Sent chat messages, pet replies that pass validation, message times and pet identifiers: retained locally on this Mac for a rolling seven-day period. Questions may remain even if no reply is produced or generation is cancelled.
  • Graduation images created from the app's pet artwork, without capturing your screen or requesting screen-recording permission.
  • Cleanup history: item names, categories, sizes, actions and times.
  • Local file identity values used to confirm external Trash deletion and prevent duplicate food credit, plus confirmed item names, paths, sizes and processing records.
  • Recovery records: operation and pet IDs, outcomes, original/destination paths and, where needed, failed paths used to distinguish unfinished record saving from completed file actions.
  • Language, visibility, size, position, movement, inspection mode, Trash retention and automatic-emptying preferences.
  • macOS bookmarks for reopening approved cache/log folders.
  • Purchase state derived from Apple product/pricing information and verified ownership or revocation information. The app keeps its own purchase state in memory while running. To offer the feedback invitation once, it temporarily saves the time of the purchase attempt or verified purchase on your Mac. It removes this local record when it presents the invitation and clears expired records when it next checks purchase status. The record contains no email address.

These records live in app-specific storage and the webview's local storage. Chat history follows the rolling seven-day retention described in section 6. Other information needed for recovery may remain; there is currently no automatic retention limit for those other records. Development builds may also keep a recent folder-access result with error codes and time. That diagnostic does not contain file contents or bookmark contents.

4. Moving and deleting files

  • Caches and logs: pressing cleanup moves eligible old items to the Trash. Moving them does not immediately free the same amount of disk space. Before emptying the Trash, you can inspect and move items back with Finder. CleanGotchi does not provide automatic restoration to their original locations.
  • Manual Trash emptying: after your confirmation, inspectable items in the internal Mac Trash are permanently deleted regardless of the selected retention period. This cannot be undone. Items that cannot be completely inspected or processed may remain.
  • Automatic Trash emptying: off by default. It operates only when a user with the paid capability enables it separately. While the app is running, it periodically permanently deletes eligible items in the internal Mac Trash meeting the chosen 10-, 30- or 100-day threshold. A waiting period applies after enabling it or changing the period, so it does not immediately delete on that change. It is not a service guaranteed to run at an exact scheduled time.

While the app runs and has Trash access, confirmed deletion of regular files from Trash within the scope above through Finder or another external action can count as food for the current pet. This observer does not read file contents, move files or delete them. It does not count restoration, movement or disk disconnection as deletion, and keeps local file identity values to avoid counting already credited files again. It does not retrospectively estimate credit for items deleted before observation was ready or while the app was closed. Access restrictions and limits on simultaneously tracked files and folders may allow only partial coverage. Symbolic links and files already having multiple hard links when observation starts are also skipped. Reconnecting a disk starts fresh observation; deletions while disconnected are not credited retrospectively. Food uses the confirmed file’s logical size, not a measurement or guarantee of physical disk space released.

If record saving fails, the app distinguishes completed file actions from records still waiting to be saved. Retrying records does not repeat those completed file actions. Further cleanup may be blocked if an earlier outcome cannot be confirmed.

5. Memory and running-app information

While the memory card is visible, the app uses macOS sysctl and vm_stat to read total memory and usage. Where permitted, it uses ps memory amounts and executable names/paths, grouping processes by app to show high-memory apps. It does not request command-line arguments or inspect a process's memory contents.

Usage may refresh approximately every 3 seconds and the app list approximately every 30 seconds. These values are used for display and short-lived in-memory caching, not persistent app records or transmission to the developer. The app does not force-quit apps or forcibly purge memory. If macOS restricts the process list, only overall memory may be shown.

6. Local pet conversations

Click the desktop pet or the dashboard’s chat button to open a small pet conversation window. When you send a message, the bundled Gemma 4 E2B text model generates the conversation reply. The app checks recognizable claims about the pet’s name, growth, food, related amounts and file actions against its current records and capabilities. If it finds a problem, it regenerates up to twice, checking a maximum of three candidates per request. Only a candidate that passes these checks is displayed. If none passes, the app keeps your input and asks you to try again. These checks cover limited app-related information, not the truth of every expression or general-knowledge statement. For questions about pet food and the free cleanup allowance when emptying the Trash in Finder or the Dock, the local model chooses a reviewed explanation in your language.

Opening the window alone does not create a reply. This feature supports Apple Silicon with macOS 14 or later; other supported Macs retain the existing pet and cleanup features. The model is included in the app, so conversations do not contact an external AI service or download a model. No separate Python installation or AI app is required.

Generation uses your typed message, selected language, current pet species and saved name, growth stage, cumulative cleanup amount counted as food, next goal and remaining amount. The app rereads this information from the saved profile for each generation and checks the pet identity, saved name and cumulative food again before returning a reply. Statements in chat are not treated as saved information. This information does not track real meals or when they were eaten. Messages are limited to 800 characters. The first candidate may use up to six recent messages, each limited to 400 characters. A short follow-up may also refer to the previous pet-record topic confirmed by the app.

When generating another candidate for the same request, the app omits earlier conversation history and uses the current question, current pet information and categories of issues detected. It does not attach the rejected candidate’s prose to the next generation. Rejected candidate replies and temporary validation/retry information are used only in memory during processing, not written to conversation files or a server. Replies that pass validation are saved in the local chat history described below.

The pet’s name is set in the dashboard and cannot be changed through chat. Current cleanup-candidate totals or item counts, filenames, full paths and file contents are not automatically attached to chat. Opening chat or sending a message does not run a new file scan. What you voluntarily type is used as input, so avoid unnecessary confidential information.

Sent messages and replies that pass validation are saved in app-specific storage on this Mac. You can reopen the conversation or restart the app and continue viewing the same pet’s recent seven-day history. Changing that pet’s name or language retains its history; a newly raised, different pet has a separate history. Records older than seven days, measured from each message’s creation time, are pruned at app startup, when history is read or written, and periodically while the app runs. No deletion runs while the app is closed; overdue records are pruned on the next launch.

Closing the conversation or changing its identity requests cancellation of an active reply and clears temporary information connecting follow-up questions. Saved history is retained separately. The reply task ends after completion, cancellation, its time limit or app exit, releasing its model memory.

The conversation has no tools for deleting/moving files or changing permissions/settings. AI mistakes can remain after these checks. Actual cleanup remains subject to the existing confirmation screens and safety checks. The app does not transmit prompts or replies to the developer or send them for model training.

9. Keeping and removing records

Removing the application file may leave app records or webview storage on your Mac. There is currently no in-app command that erases every record. To remove local records, quit the app, identify its app-specific storage and back up anything you need before deleting it. Do not confuse app records with your cache, log or Trash folders. For help, contact pxlsekp@gmail.com.

Deleting app records does not undo file moves or permanent deletions. Apple manages purchase records separately, and removing local records does not cancel an Apple purchase. System permissions such as Full Disk Access are also managed separately.
